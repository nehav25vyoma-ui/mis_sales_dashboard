22. KPI SPARKLINE ENHANCEMENT
-----------------------------

Enhance the sparkline inside every KPI card:

1. Add a delta badge beside the headline showing latest-period versus
   previous-period percentage change.
   - Positive: green with up-arrow styling.
   - Negative: orange/red.
   - No change or insufficient prior data: neutral gray with "—".
   - Format percentages to one decimal place, such as +7.2% or -3.1%.

2. Match sparkline color to direction:
   - Green for upward trend.
   - Orange/red for downward trend.
   - Gray for flat or neutral.

3. Display one solid marker only on the final data point. Its color must match
   the line.

4. Keep the chart minimal:
   - No axes.
   - No gridlines.
   - No legend.

5. For all-zero or insufficient trends, show a flat dashed line or an em dash
   rather than a misleading moving line.

6. Synchronize trend and delta with channel filters:
   - All Channels uses the combined trend.
   - A selected channel uses only its own trend.

The time series should contain at least two periods for delta calculation and
ideally six or more for a meaningful trend shape.


23. INDEPENDENT TIME-GRAIN FILTER
---------------------------------

Add a second independent KPI Dashboard filter for time grain alongside the
channel filter.

Time-grain options:

- Monthly
- Quarterly
- Yearly

Default to Monthly, or the grain with the most complete available data.

Both filters apply together. For example, Website Sales plus Quarterly shows
Website Sales data aggregated by quarter.

Aggregation:

- Monthly: one point per month.
- Quarterly: sum the three months in each calendar quarter.
- Yearly: sum all months in each year.
- Filter by channel first, then aggregate by time grain.

Sparkline and delta:

- Plot all aggregated points available for the selected grain.
- Delta always compares the latest aggregated point with the immediately
  previous aggregated point at that grain.
- If fewer than two aggregated points exist, hide both sparkline and delta
  badge.
- Display "Not enough data at this grain yet" instead.

Card dimensions must remain fixed regardless of filter selection or data
volume.

Show Channel and Time Grain as two clearly labelled, independent control
groups.

Keep monthly data as the single source of truth and derive quarterly/yearly
aggregations rather than storing separate copies.
